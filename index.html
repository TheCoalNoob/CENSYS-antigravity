CENSYS is a thesis project developed as a LoRa mesh-based community fire monitoring network designed to protect barangays in Cebu City. By integrating real-time IoT sensor nodes, multi-hop LoRa communication, and a geo-mapped emergency dashboard, CENSYS provides early fire detection and instant alerts to fire stations — ensuring faster response times and safer communities.
